Current research

BRAC University · Undergraduate thesis in progress

The Unlearning Illusion

The Unlearning Illusion: A Red-Teaming Framework for Probing the Reversibility of Machine Unlearning in Large Language Models.

I’m developing adversarial recovery tests to distinguish apparent forgetting from genuine removal. The question is whether information targeted for removal remains recoverable under red-team probing. I build Python experiment and analysis pipelines for systematic comparison.

Independent AI safety research · 2026 - Present

Monitor Calibration Transport

Monitor Calibration Transport: Evaluating AI Safety Monitor Reliability Across Models and Distribution Shifts.

I investigate whether safety monitors calibrated on one model or task remain calibrated across models, task distributions and deployment-like shifts. The focus is false-negative risk, transportability and the reliability of monitoring signals.

Daud Ibrahim Hassan · 2026 · Independent preprint

MarkLens

I built a reproducible SEC EDGAR pipeline that extracted 251,350 position-level holdings and constructed 2,163 same-loan, same-date cross-manager facility-quarters.

Matching was validated at 97.6% precision and 93.5% recall. Cross-manager valuation disagreement was 1.41 points of par, with a 95% confidence interval of 1.13-1.74. The code, panel dataset, analysis and Observatory were released with a DOI.
